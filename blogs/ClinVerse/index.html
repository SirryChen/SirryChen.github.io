    How are the five metrics defined?
    ICR · Information Collection Rate
    Weighted coverage of initially undisclosed patient-reportable information; key, supporting, and background units receive weights of 3, 2, and 1.
    TOC · Test Order Coverage
    Coverage of distinct, initially hidden case-supported laboratory, microbiology, and radiology findings. Repeated requests do not increase coverage.
    ATC · Abnormal Test Coverage
    Coverage of initially hidden abnormal laboratory and microbiology findings.
    DCA / DSA · Diagnostic agreement
    ICD-10 block match / three-character category match. Single-case scores are binary; the table reports aggregate accuracy.
